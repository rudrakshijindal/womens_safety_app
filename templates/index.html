Built around trust

Every part of the network is designed so that only reviewed people can respond, and so that private details stay private.

Approved by admin

Guardians apply with their details. They can respond to requests only after an administrator approves them.

Nearest first

Approved guardians who are on duty are ranked by distance using the Haversine formula. Only approximate distance is shown.

Clear status

Requests move from pending to accepted to resolved, and the person who asked for help sees each change.

What this prototype does

  • Creates in-app alerts and notifications
  • Matches guardians by real distance calculation
  • Stores every response with a timestamp
  • Keeps passwords hashed and forms CSRF protected

What it does not do

  • It does not send SMS or make phone calls
  • It does not contact the police
  • It does not track location in the background
  • Sample guardians use simulated locations
