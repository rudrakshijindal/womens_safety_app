Send an SOS request?

This creates an emergency request inside this app and matches it to approved guardians who are on duty near you.

When you confirm, your browser will ask to share your location. If you decline, or location is unavailable, a simulated demo location is used and the request is clearly labelled as simulated.

Demo status: no SMS, call or police alert is sent.
